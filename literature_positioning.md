# Literature positioning

The project is positioned relative to several established directions:

- stochastic modified-equation and diffusion approximations of SGD;
- Hessian and projected-gradient-noise analyses;
- finite-step and discrete SGD dynamics;
- operator and Koopman analyses of algorithms.

Accordingly, the repository does not treat the existence of stochastic corrections, discrete transition operators, noise anisotropy, or generic operator spectra as novel by themselves.

The experimentally supported contribution is narrower: quantitative held-out prediction of finite-step conditional statistics and finite-horizon covariance from locally measurable stochastic geometry, together with explicit falsification gates and validity restrictions.
