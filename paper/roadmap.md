# Paper Roadmap

## Minimum publishable package

1. Fully checked finite-sum theorem and exact threshold formula.
2. M9 controlled comparison with leakage audit and uncertainty reporting.
3. Genuine finite-sum/minibatch replication of the noise-geometry effect.
4. Literature table separating the claim from Liao, Ignashin, multiplicative-noise stability, and stochastic modified equations.
5. Public reproducibility script for every figure/table included in the manuscript.

## Stronger package

6. Multidimensional noncommuting theorem or exact control experiment.
7. Held-out neural prediction showing incremental value over GNS, sharpness, `eta*lambda_max`, and projected covariance.
8. Robust estimator for the local noise-geometry descriptor with finite-sample uncertainty.

## Stop conditions

Do not submit the strongest descriptor-insufficiency narrative if the genuine minibatch replication fails or if the literature audit finds the exact finite-sum theorem already established in equivalent form.
