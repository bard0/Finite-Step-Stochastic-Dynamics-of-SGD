# Paper roadmap

Working direction:

**Temporal stochastic geometry of finite-step SGD**

## Required evidence chain

1. Validate the local stochastic mechanism.
2. Establish finite-horizon covariance prediction.
3. Demonstrate temporal-ordering effects under matched marginal controls.
4. Test robustness across architectures and datasets.
5. Define theoretical validity boundaries.

## Required limitations

The paper should avoid universal claims and clearly state assumptions:

- finite-step regime;
- local approximations;
- tested architectures and datasets;
- observable-dependent conclusions.
