# Paper Roadmap

## Minimum publishable package

1. Fully checked finite-sum theorem and exact threshold formula.
2. M9 controlled comparison with leakage audit and uncertainty reporting.
3. Genuine finite-sum/minibatch replication of the noise-geometry effect.
4. Literature table separating the claim from Liao, Ignashin, multiplicative-noise stability, and stochastic modified equations.
5. Public reproducibility script for every figure/table included in the manuscript.
6. Explicit neural-transfer limitation: G8.10-B4 failed the preregistered strong regime-robust incremental-value test for `chi_mix`.

## Stronger package

7. Multidimensional noncommuting theorem or exact control experiment.
8. A new neural result only if a narrowly preregistered hypothesis survives independent prospective confirmation after the B4 diagnostic stage.
9. Robust uncertainty-aware estimators for any local stochastic-geometry descriptor used in a neural claim.

## Stop conditions

- Do not submit the strongest descriptor-insufficiency narrative if genuine minibatch replication fails or if the literature audit finds the exact finite-sum theorem already established in equivalent form.
- Do not use eta-stratified B4 observations as confirmatory evidence.
- Do not make Liao-mechanistic claims from the B4 secondary subset while applicability attrition remains unresolved.
