# Claims and Evidence

## Confirmed

Claims directly supported by validated experiments.

Current confirmed areas:

- finite-horizon covariance prediction mechanisms in tested regimes;
- controlled validation of stochastic operator effects.

## Numerically supported

Quantitative evidence exists, but broader validation is required.

## Exploratory

Current research hypotheses:

1. Temporal stochastic structure may provide predictive information beyond matched marginal statistics.
2. Common edge-of-stability descriptors may not fully determine finite-step SGD dynamics.
3. Discrete SGD operators may contain information not captured by continuous approximations.

## Falsification tests

- Construct systems with matched descriptors and different finite-step operators.
- Test held-out prediction against descriptor-only baselines.
- Compare against Langevin and stochastic modified equation approximations.

## Principle

Negative results and failed hypotheses are preserved because they constrain the theory.
