# Research roadmap

## Current priority

1. Preserve the M9 noise-geometry result as the strongest positive controlled result and replicate it in genuine finite-sum/minibatch settings.
2. Complete the cheap diagnostic of the G8.10-B4 eta heterogeneity without changing the failed primary verdict.
3. Only if a stable regime-dependent effect survives influence, residualization, collinearity, and negative-control checks, preregister a small independent neural confirmation.
4. Develop multidimensional noncommuting controls with exact second-moment/Lyapunov baselines.
5. Keep higher-order theory tied to measurable consequences and explicit literature boundaries.

## Current empirical direction

The main neural question is no longer whether `chi_mix` can be measured; that feasibility gate has passed. The question is whether any higher-order descriptor has reproducible incremental held-out value beyond lower-order stochastic, curvature, finite-step, and rotation baselines.

The first strong regime-robust G8.10-B4 test failed. Therefore the next step is diagnosis, not escalation.

## Required baselines

- sharpness / top Hessian eigenvalue;
- `eta*lambda_max` stability measures;
- projected gradient-noise variance and Liao-style local quantities when applicable;
- verified finite-step/random-Hessian descriptors;
- eigenspace-rotation correction;
- negative-control descriptors.

## Theory program

Priority:

- exact finite-step operator / lifted-moment analysis;
- descriptor sufficiency and matched constructions;
- higher-order finite-horizon expansions only where they produce distinct observables;
- literature comparison with Liao et al. 2026, Ignashin et al. 2026, multiplicative-noise stability, stochastic modified equations, and weak stochastic expansions.

## Stop rules

- Do not reinterpret the B4 primary failure post hoc.
- Do not launch a larger neural experiment solely because one eta stratum looks favorable.
- Do not promote a transformed `chi_mix` descriptor unless it is fixed before a new independent target acquisition.
- Stop the mixed-fourth predictive branch if the existing-data diagnostic collapses under influence/residualization or is matched by the frozen random control.
- Do not claim universal stochastic geometry, a universal spectral law, or a new optimizer without separate evidence.
