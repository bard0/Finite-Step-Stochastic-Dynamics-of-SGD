# M2 provenance

## Purpose

M2 investigates the role of temporal stochastic organization in finite-horizon covariance dynamics.

## Required reproducibility records

- sampling procedure;
- temporal ordering protocol;
- marginal statistics;
- covariance measurements;
- random seeds;
- evaluation configuration.

## Scientific controls

The interpretation relies on comparisons where one-step statistics are controlled while temporal organization is varied.

## Claim boundary

The experiment tests a specific causal mechanism in a controlled setting. It is not presented as a universal theory of stochastic optimization.
