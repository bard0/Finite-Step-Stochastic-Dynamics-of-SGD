# M2 results summary

## Question

Does temporal organization of stochastic gradients provide additional information about finite-horizon covariance geometry beyond matched one-step statistics?

## Experimental idea

M2 compares stochastic processes with controlled one-step statistics while changing temporal organization.

The central object is the temporal covariance structure:

\[
K_{st}=\operatorname{Cov}(\xi_s,\xi_t).
\]

## Interpretation

A positive result supports the statement that temporal structure can influence finite-horizon stochastic evolution in the tested setting.

## Claim boundary

M2 does not claim discovery of a universal memory mechanism for SGD. The result is limited to the controlled experimental regime and requires further validation across models and datasets.
