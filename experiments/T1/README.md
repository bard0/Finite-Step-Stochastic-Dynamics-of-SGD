# T1 — Temporal covariance theory validation

## Objective

Formalize the contribution of temporal gradient-noise covariance to finite-horizon stochastic dynamics.

## Central object

The theoretical object is the temporal covariance kernel:

`K_st = Cov(xi_s, xi_t)`

and its propagation through finite-step dynamics.

## Research role

T1 connects controlled causal experiments with the mathematical formulation of temporal stochastic geometry.

## Open questions

- assumptions required for approximation validity;
- error bounds;
- scaling beyond local linearization;
- relation to existing finite-step SGD analyses.

## Status

Planned/theory validation extension. Claims should remain restricted until supported by experiments.
